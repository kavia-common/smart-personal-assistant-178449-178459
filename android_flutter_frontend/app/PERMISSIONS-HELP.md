# Permissions Help

Why does the app request these permissions?

- Location: Used to provide contextual insights and nearby recommendations. The app doesn't access your location in the background at this stage.
- Media (Photos): Used to analyze images locally for insights on spend patterns and wellness. No media leaves your device.
- SMS (optional): Used only if you opt in. The app analyzes messages locally to detect transaction-like summaries for spend insights. You can revoke this anytime.

If you permanently denied a permission:
- Tap "Open System Settings" under the respective section to review and grant the permission.
